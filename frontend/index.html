FIELD GUIDE

How to Photograph Leaves for AI Diagnosis

Follow these visual guidelines to ensure reliable AI inference and agricultural evaluation.

DO THIS (RECOMMENDED)
  • Single Leaf Close-Up: Position camera 15–25 cm from the leaf so leaf lesions fill the view.
  • Both Sides of Leaf: For suspected rust or mildew, photograph both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
  • Diffuse Daylight: Early morning or overcast daylight prevents harsh glare on glossy leaves.
  • Steady Focus: Tap your phone screen to lock focus onto leaf spot borders.
AVOID THIS
  • Wide Field Shots: Whole plants or field rows do not provide enough pixel detail for leaf lesions.
  • Heavy Motion Blur: Blurry leaf veins lead to ambiguous low-confidence predictions.
  • Hands Over Symptoms: Do not cover lesions with fingers or clip holders.
  • Severe Night Flash: Artificial flash washes out fine discoloration patterns.
ABOUT AGRIINTEL

AI-Powered Agricultural Intelligence

AgriIntel combines computer vision and curated agricultural knowledge to help farmers, researchers, and extension officers make fast, informed decisions.

🌾 Mission & Responsible AI

Democratizing agricultural AI for smallholder farmers in India and beyond. Our AI provides a first-opinion diagnosis to help users know when to seek expert assistance — never to replace it.

Built on the PlantVillage benchmark dataset with additional rice and wheat disease imagery from IRRI and ICAR. Integrated SerpApi ensures live, current advisory information.

Always consult a local agricultural officer (KVK) before applying treatments.
